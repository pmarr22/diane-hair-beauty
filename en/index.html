About

Diane, Brazilian hairdresser in Le Cannet.

Revealing the natural beauty of every strand.

Diane moved from Brazil to France in 2012, but she had already been working as a hairdresser in Brazil: almost 20 years in the profession in total. In France she added the CAP and BP diplomas and a real passion for colour. After living in Paris, she opened her own salon in Le Cannet about 3 years ago, to give clients a warm moment, with the energy and know-how of Brazil.

She styles women, men and children, all hair types, and also trains other professionals in balayage, colourimetry and extensions.

Do you speak English and Portuguese?

Yes! Diane and the team welcome you in English, français and português (Brasil).

Where can I park?

There is a car park right in front of the salon, with the first hour free. Public transport stops within 500 m.

What are your opening hours?

Monday and Tuesday 9am–6pm, Thursday and Friday 9am–7pm, Saturday 9:30am–7pm. Closed Wednesday and Sunday.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes: 3x or 4x interest-free, or up to 10x by card, from €150.

What if I’m late or need to cancel?

We tolerate 10 minutes of delay. For late cancellations or no-shows, 20% of the service price is retained. You can change or cancel from your Planity account.

How much is Brazilian smoothing?

From €170 for short hair (about 2h15). The final price depends on length and thickness: send a photo for a free opinion.
