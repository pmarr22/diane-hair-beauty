About

Diane, Brazilian hairdresser in Le Cannet.

Revealing the natural beauty of every strand.

Diane moved from Brazil to France in 2012, but she had already been working as a hairdresser in Brazil: almost 20 years in the profession in total. In France she added the CAP and BP diplomas and a real passion for colour. After living in Paris, she opened her own salon in Le Cannet about 3 years ago, to give clients a warm moment, with the energy and know-how of Brazil.

She styles women, men and children, all hair types, and also trains other professionals in balayage, colourimetry and extensions.
